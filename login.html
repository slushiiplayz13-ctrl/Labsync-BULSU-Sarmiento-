Laboratory Management & Monitoring System

About the System

LabSync is a unified, smart scheduling and monitoring system built specifically for the Computer Laboratories of Bulacan State University - Sarmiento Campus. By integrating real-time scheduling, digitized PC issue reporting via QR codes, and seamless communication channels between Faculty, IT Department Heads, and MIS Staff, LabSync aims to optimize laboratory uptime and streamline academic operations.

Laboratory Policies & Terms of Service

Institutional Governance & Compliance

LabSync operates in full compliance with the Bulacan State University - Sarmiento Campus Laboratory Code of Conduct and the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). All students, faculty members, and administrative personnel must adhere to the policies governing computer workstation access, resource allocation, and digital accountability.

Core Laboratory Principles

1. Authorized Academic Use

Workstations and network privileges are reserved solely for curriculum-related laboratory activities, programming, research, and scheduled classes.

2. Hardware & Peripherals Integrity

Disconnecting, swapping, or tampering with PC components, peripherals, cables, or power supplies is strictly prohibited. Report issues immediately.

3. Privacy & Session Accountability

Under RA 10173, user activity logs, QR schedule scans, and maintenance reports are safeguarded. Users must log out and never share credentials.

4. Key Custody & Room Security

Laboratory key claims and transfers are digitally logged with timestamps. Custodians are responsible for ensuring physical room security.
