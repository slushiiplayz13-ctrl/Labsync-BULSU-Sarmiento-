1. Purpose & Scope of the System

LabSync is the dedicated, smart laboratory resource management and workstation monitoring infrastructure deployed across the Computer Laboratories of Bulacan State University – Sarmiento Campus (BulSU-SC).

This system regulates and audits the following campus operations:

  • Student Workstation Issue Reporting: Zero-login, mobile-first defect submissions via durable QR code labels affixed to lab computers.
  • Faculty & Class Laboratory Scheduling: Automated timetable management and conflict-free room reservations.
  • IoT Smart Key Cabinet & Physical Key Circulation: Profile QR-authenticated key dispatching (scanning the faculty member's personal profile QR code on the key cabinet), strict faculty key accountability, and digital peer-to-peer key transfer handovers.
  • MIS Technical Maintenance: Digital repair ticketing, component diagnostics, technician assignment, and resolution logging.
By interacting with any LabSync interface—including scanning workstation QR codes, submitting issue reports, claiming laboratory keys, or operating system dashboards—you acknowledge and agree to comply with this Policy and the BulSU Student/Faculty Code of Conduct.

2. Workstation QR Fault Reporting Policy

LabSync provides zero-friction fault reporting so students and faculty can immediately report hardware or software malfunctions without administrative barriers. To ensure system reliability and prompt technical repair:

  • Honest & Accurate Submissions: Users must report genuine, directly observed defects (e.g., monitor no-display, defective mouse/keyboard, system unit power failure, or operating system errors).
  • Verification Confirmation: Before submission, the reporting student must confirm that they are currently occupying the specified PC unit and that their report accurately reflects the workstation's condition.
  • Prohibition of Ticket Spamming & Fraud: Submitting fictitious fault reports, flooding duplicate tickets, or reporting functional equipment as broken disrupts laboratory scheduling and MIS maintenance. Repeat fraudulent submissions will be reported to the College Disciplinary Committee.
  • Physical Equipment Protection: Students must exercise due care with laboratory peripherals. Any intentional physical tampering, hardware detachment, or vandalism is strictly penalized under University rules.

4. Physical Key & Smart Cabinet Accountability

Laboratory rooms contain sensitive institutional assets and technology equipment. The following rules govern all physical keys circulating under the LabSync ecosystem:

  • Authorized Possession: Only faculty members with verified active teaching schedules or authorized MIS personnel may claim laboratory room keys from the Smart Key Cabinet by scanning their personal profile QR code on the cabinet's optical scanner.
  • Immutable Audit Trails: Every key transaction—including profile QR authorization, solenoid unlock, key extraction, key return, and peer-to-peer transfer—is logged with cryptographic timestamps in the system database.
  • Peer-to-Peer Transfer Protocol: If an instructor passes a room key to the succeeding instructor, both parties must execute the digital transfer via key-transfer.html to reassign accountability. Handing off keys without digital recording leaves the original holder accountable for any subsequent room incidents.
  • Immediate Return Duty: Keys must be returned to the Smart Cabinet or designated custodian immediately after the conclusion of scheduled laboratory classes. Holding keys overnight without explicit IT Head approval is strictly prohibited.

5. Data Retention, Disposal & User Rights

Under the provisions of the Philippine Data Privacy Act, data subjects hold specific statutory rights regarding their personal data processed by LabSync:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know how your information is collected, stored, and utilized.
  • Right to Access & Rectify: You may request review or correction of inaccurate records (e.g., mistaken student name or erroneous key log) through the MIS Office.
  • Data Retention Period: Workstation defect tickets and routine key logs are retained for a maximum of one (1) Academic Year for operational analytics, equipment warranty claims, and accreditation auditing. Historical logs are systematically archived or anonymized thereafter.
